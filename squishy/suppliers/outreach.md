# Supplier outreach (Phase 0)

Send from the business email once the entity exists. Replace [BRAND] and [NAME]. Ask every supplier the same six questions so quotes are comparable; paste answers into `config/assortment.json` (`unit_cost`, `quote_status: "quoted"`) and `ops/compliance_tracker.json` (CPC details).

## The six questions (every supplier)

1. Wholesale price list and price breaks (we expect 20–200 units per SKU for a first order).
2. Minimum order per SKU and per order; opening-order minimum.
3. Lead time from payment to delivery to a US address.
4. Children's Product Certificate and CPSC-accepted lab test reports (ASTM F963-23, lead, phthalates) for each SKU, dated within the last 12 months; confirmation there is no water-bead or liquid-gel fill.
5. Minimum advertised price policy, and whether we may sell the item inside multi-brand bundles and blind bags.
6. Whether product photos and video may be used in our listings and TikTok content, and whether they run an affiliate or co-op programme.

## 1. Faire (marketplace, apply as retailer)

Apply at faire.com/retailer with the business name, EIN, website (a live Shopify URL with a coming-soon page is enough) and a short description: "Online curated squishy and sensory gift boutique launching Q4 2026, US-only shipping." Faire offers net-60 terms and free returns on opening orders with many brands, which suits a validation launch. Search brands: ORB Toys, Schylling, Anboor, Toysmith, Top Trenz, Kawaii Company, Iscream.

## Red flags that disqualify a supplier

- No CPC or "we can make one" (a CPC must be backed by a real lab report).
- Character likenesses (Sanrio, Pokémon, Stitch, Kirby) without a licence.
- Egg-shaped plush with embroidered faces and belly patches (Squishmallows trade dress).
- Water beads, liquid gel, or "maltose" jelly fills from unbranded factories.
- Refusal to state the fill material or age grade in writing.
