# Legal and safety copy blocks

These blocks are inserted by `shopify/setup_store.py` into product descriptions, a Safety page, and the footer. Wording follows 16 CFR 1500.19/1500.20 (choking warning), California Prop 65 short-form rules (post-2025 format naming a chemical), and CPSC children's-product requirements. A product-safety lawyer should confirm Prop 65 chemical names per SKU once test reports are in.

## Choking hazard (every product page, principal display panel of every pack)

> **WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD — Small parts. Not for children under 3 yrs.**

Use on every SKU whose product or any component fits the small-parts cylinder (1.25 in x 2.25 in). In this assortment that is all of them. Age grade shown on listing: 6+.

## Prop 65 short form (product page + pack, California shipments)

> **⚠ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — [chemical name, e.g. DEHP / Di(2-ethylhexyl)phthalate] – www.P65Warnings.ca.gov**

Fill the chemical per SKU from the lab report or supplier declaration. If tests show none of the listed chemicals above safe-harbour levels, the warning may be omitted for that SKU; record the decision in `ops/compliance_tracker.json`.

## Safety page (site page "Safety & Testing")

Every product we sell is a certified children's product. That means each item has been tested by a CPSC-accepted laboratory to ASTM F963-23 (the US toy safety standard), for lead and for phthalates, and carries a Children's Product Certificate from its manufacturer or importer. We keep those certificates on file and will share the certificate number for any item on request.

We do not sell water-bead or liquid-gel squishies from unbranded sources. We do not sell items that imitate licensed characters. We age-grade every item 6+ and label small parts.

Squishies are not food and are not for pets. Do not microwave, freeze or cut them open.

## Age and audience statement (About page)

Our products are toys under the Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act and are treated as children's products regardless of how they are marketed. Our advertising is directed to adults 18 and over.

## Privacy note (Privacy page addition)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our newsletter, reviews and account features are for adults 18 and over.

## Returns policy summary

Unopened items may be returned within 30 days for a refund of the item price. Opened squishies cannot be resold for hygiene reasons; if an item arrives damaged or is not as described, send a photo within 7 days and we will replace it or refund it without a return.

## Shipping policy summary

Orders ship from the United States within 1–2 business days by USPS Ground Advantage. Free shipping on orders of $35 or more; $4.95 below that. Holiday order-by dates are posted on the home page in December.

## Trademark hygiene (internal)

Never use the words Squishmallow(s), Squishable(s), Squishlings, Pop It, NeeDoh (except for the genuine Schylling product listed as such), Labubu, or any licensed character name in product titles, tags, alt text, ads or hashtags other than the genuine branded item.
