# Store policies (paste into Shopify admin: Settings → Policies)

The connector cannot write legal policies (scope `write_legal_policies` is not granted), so paste these by hand. Each fills one box on the Policies page.

## Refund policy

Unopened items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Opened squishies cannot be resold for hygiene reasons and are not returnable. If an item arrives damaged or is not as described, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it or refund it without requiring a return. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Return shipping for unopened items is paid by the customer unless the item was our error.

## Shipping policy

We ship within the United States only. Orders ship from the United States within 1–2 business days by USPS Ground Advantage, typically arriving in 2–5 business days. Shipping is free on orders of $29.99 or more and $4.95 below that. Tracking is emailed when the label is created. Holiday order-by dates are posted on the home page in December. We are not responsible for delays caused by the carrier or by an incorrect address entered at checkout.

## Privacy policy

Deskloaf collects the information needed to process and ship your order: name, shipping and billing address, email, phone number, and payment details handled by our payment processor. We use your email to send order updates and, if you opt in, marketing messages you can unsubscribe from at any time. We use cookies and similar technologies for site analytics and, where permitted, advertising measurement. We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the store (payment processing, shipping, email delivery, analytics).

Our store is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; our newsletter, reviews and account features are for adults 18 and over. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and California residents may exercise their rights under the CCPA, by contacting us at the store email address. The Your Privacy Choices link in the footer allows you to opt out of data sharing.

## Terms of service

Use Shopify's generated template (Settings → Policies → Create from template) and add this line under "Products": "All products are toys intended for ages 6 and up unless stated otherwise. Do not microwave, freeze or cut open squishies."
