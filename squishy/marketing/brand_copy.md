# Deskloaf brand copy (v1, 20 Sept 2026)

Applied to the theme's home, about and announcement sections by `shopify/setup_store.py` once the store is connected. Voice: calm, dry, a little funny; never babyish. Written for adults; never addresses children directly (advertising is 18+).

**Name logic.** A loaf is what a cat does when it tucks in and goes still. Deskloaf is the squishy that sits on your desk and does the same, until you pick it up.

## Home: three value blocks
- **Curated, not dumped.** Three pieces that go together, picked by hand. No thirty-pack of random mochi.
- **Certified.** Every item is a tested children's product with a certificate on file. No water beads, no gel dupes, no knock-off characters.
- **Made for desks, stolen by kids.** Ages 6 and up. We just assume the adult ordered it for themselves.

## Home: how a taba works (short explainer block)
Taba squishies are cast silicone, not foam. They stick slightly to your fingers, stretch, and rebound slowly, and unlike foam they don't crack or lose their rise. That's why we lead with them.

## About page
Deskloaf started as a drawer of squishies next to a laptop. The 2026 squish wave brought a flood of thirty-packs, gel dupes and recalls, and it got hard to find a few good ones from makers who test their products. So we pick them for you: silicone tabas, slow-rise pastries, the occasional genuine NeeDoh, bundled in threes and shipped free from the US.

We only stock items that carry a Children's Product Certificate and current lab reports. Our advertising is directed to adults; our products are age-graded 6+.

## Audience-led visual identity (v2, 20 Sept 2026)

Primary buyer: women 24–38 buying for their own desk. Secondary: parents gifting 7–12s; adult blind-bag collectors. Copy addresses the self-buyer; the others overhear.

**Palette:** cream #FFF6EC ground, paper #FFFDF8 cards, plum ink #3B2233, blush #FFD6E0, peach #FFB88C, sage #BFD8B8, butter #FFE8A3, lilac #DCCBFF, coral pop #FF5A6E (one accent for buttons and highlights).
**Type:** Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headlines, Manrope for body. Chunky outlines (3px plum) and 5px offset shadows for a sticker feel without reading as toy-aisle.
**Copy rules:** name the moment (9:04 AM, 3:02 PM, 11:20 PM), give permission ("Yes, it's for you"), lead with texture and camera-worthiness, keep safety as a reason to trust rather than a warning tone. Parents get one clearly labelled gift strip.
**Mockup:** `squishy/marketing/site/deskloaf-mockup.html` (published artifact). Sections: marquee, hero with squish demo, ritual band, bundles, "your desk three ways", Taba 101 with rebound meter, safety ("two were recalled this month"), gift strip, signup, legal footer, mobile buy bar.
