Taba 101

Silicone that squishes like dough and comes back every time.

Taba squishies are cast silicone, not foam. Slightly tacky, stretchy, slow to rebound. Foam dries out and cracks by spring. Silicone doesn't. That's why the capybara is silicone and only the pastries are foam.
