# Deskloaf content calendar: pre-launch and launch (2 weeks)

Cadence: 1 TikTok per day minimum, cross-posted to Instagram Reels and Facebook. Instagram grid gets 3 stills a week. Every video is under 20 seconds, vertical, captions burned in, real product on camera. Claude writes hooks, scripts and captions; the human shoots 10–20 minutes of raw footage per week in one sitting.

Rules: advertising is directed to adults, never address children; no licensed characters in frame; no "non-toxic" claims without a report; never use the blocked words in `brand_copy.md`.

## Filming kit (one-time, under $60)
Phone, a $15 clip-on ring light, a $20 tabletop tripod with phone mount, a plain desk surface, a white A3 card as a bounce, a lapel mic if the phone mic picks up room echo. Shoot near a window in daylight. Overhead and 45-degree angles only.

## Footage bank to shoot in the first session (before stock arrives, use the sample units)
1. Ten-second slow squeeze of each product, overhead, no hands leaving frame.
2. The "rise": press flat, release, hold still while it recovers.
3. Bundle unboxing: mailer, sticker sheet, three items lined up.
4. Desk context: squishy next to keyboard, in a coat pocket, on a bag clip.
5. Hands-only "tier list" arranging squishies on the desk.
6. Bad-example B-roll: a thirty-pack of random mochi dumped on the desk (for the "curated, not dumped" contrast).

## Instagram grid (3 stills a week)
Product on plain desk, overhead, one item per image, consistent cream/peach backdrop. Alternate: product still, lifestyle (desk, bag, pocket), text card with one line from the brand copy.

## Caption formula
One line of hook, one line of fact (price, material, "ships free"), one call to action. Hashtags: five to eight, always including #deskloaf and #squishy; rotate #tabasquishy #squishtok #fidget #desksetup #asmr #capybara.

## Weekly loop (Claude runs, human shoots)
- Sunday: Claude drafts next week's seven hooks and scripts from what performed (views at 3 s, completion rate, saves).
- Monday: human shoots in one 20-minute session from the shot list.
- Daily: post at 6–8 pm local; reply to every comment within 24 h.
- Gate metric: one post over 100K views or one affiliate driving 20+ orders by 15 Nov.
