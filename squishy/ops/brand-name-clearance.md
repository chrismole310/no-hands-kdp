- **What worked:** web search (search-engine snippets of Justia/Trademarkia records), GitHub profile fetches, and DNS resolution from the sandbox.
- **Domain method used:** DNS resolution as a proxy. `NXDOMAIN` for the apex and `www` strongly implies unregistered (registered .coms almost always have NS delegation), but it is not proof. A resolving A record means it is registered (parked or live).
- **Trademark method used:** search-engine-indexed USPTO records via Justia/Trademarkia, for the exact name and obvious confusables. This is a knockout screen, not a clearance search. No status below was confirmed against live TSDR.
- **Handles:** direct checks impossible. "Unknown" means no search-engine evidence of an account either way.

## Ranked top 3

1. **Deskloaf**: safest by a clear margin. Zero results in any context (no business, product, social account or mark), the .com and .co/.shop/.store all appear unregistered, 8 letters, one word, easy to spell and say. "Loaf" is suggestive, not descriptive, of silicone squishies, and it hits the brief: desk + cozy + bread/cat-loaf motif + "loafing around" calm. Biggest unknown is the TikTok/IG handle.

2. **Doughlet**: clean on every check that could run. Strong fit for jumbo food squishies. An attorney must weigh phonetic similarity to the PLAY-DOH family (Hasbro) and NEEDOH (Schylling); "Doughlet" shares no spelling with those, but a sound-alike argument is possible.

3. **Fidgetnook**: clean on registrations and the .com looks free, but weakest as a brand: "fidget" is descriptive and hard to enforce, the phrase is already in loose use, 10 letters, and it shares NOOK with a famous mark. Fallback only. If the identity leans hard on capybaras, Capynap fits better but collides with Showcase's existing "CapyNap" silicone product.

## What a human must still verify before committing

1. **Paid USPTO clearance / attorney opinion** for the chosen name in Class 28 (toys) and Class 35 (retail): exact mark, phonetic equivalents (DESK LOAF, LOAF-formative marks; DOUGHLET, DOHLET, and the DOH family), design marks, state registrations. The screen above relied on search snippets; statuses may be stale.
2. **Buy the .com immediately** at a registrar and confirm availability there. Also take .co/.shop and `shop<name>` variants if cheap.
3. **Register TikTok, Instagram and X handles the same day** and check them by hand.
4. **Common-law search** on Etsy, Amazon, TikTok Shop and Shopify stores for the exact name.
5. **File an intent-to-use (1(b)) application** in Classes 28 and 35 once cleared; run a state business-name/DBA check.
