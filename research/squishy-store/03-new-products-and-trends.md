# Squishy & Sensory Product Trend Scout — September 2026

**Method note.** 43 web searches were run. Full-page fetches were blocked for every publisher tried (CNBC, Circana, Toy Book, CPSC, Wikipedia, Alibaba, etc.), so figures below come from search-result snippets rather than full articles. Treat exact numbers as "reported" and verify before putting them in a deck. Where evidence is thin it is flagged.

## 1. The big picture (Sept 2026): squishies are at peak, not emerging

- **Circana**: US squishy-toy sales posted *triple-digit* dollar and unit growth through April 2026; H1 2026 squishy sales roughly **quadrupled YoY to ~$297M**. The whole US toy industry was +13% dollars through April, with adults (18+) driving 35% of growth. Circana names **NeeDoh** as the spark.
- **Faire (Aug 2026)**: independent-retailer "Stuffed & Plush" orders +628% YoY in Q2, ~75% of it squishies; squishy orders average ~25 units vs ~6 for other kids' items; searches for "NeeDoh" and "Dumplings" +400%, spiking ~6 weeks before mainstream press. Squishies are now >60% of kids-category volume even in hardware stores, pharmacies and grocers.
- **Press framing has flipped to "craze"**: CNBC (27 Aug 2026) "The toy industry's latest craze is cheap, colorful and very squishy"; KMUW (21 Aug) "latest in a long line of toy crazes"; NBC/Hola "Is NeeDoh the new Labubu?".
- **Regulatory/legal overhang**: CPSC issued an Aug 2026 alert on fake/counterfeit squishies and burn hazards (355,683 units stopped at ports, 55 shipments); recalls of water-bead "Squeezy Dumplings" and OKK "Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun"; UK recall of a Squeezy Dumpling for benzene; Forbes (14 Jul) on the microwave "hack" sending kids to burn units; a **proposed class action vs Schylling (NeeDoh) filed 21 Jul 2026** (N.D. Cal., case conference 21 Oct 2026). Gel-filled formats are the exposure point.
- **Interpretation**: Q4 2026 will be the *largest* squishy holiday ever (Toy Insider's Hot 20 / 12 Under $20 lists launch 23 Sep and explicitly feature NeeDoh and "toys that squish"), but commodity gel squishies are late-cycle and legally risky. The defensible plays are silicone "taba"-style, PU food/slow-rise with strong characters, capybara/kidult positioning, blind-box mechanics and personalization.

## 3. TikTok / TikTok Shop signals

- TikTok Shop is now ~1% of US retail and ~3% of e-commerce; toys/hobbies/collectibles is its 3rd-largest category (Circana).
- Content formats driving sales: ASMR squeeze clips, "NeeDoh hunting"/restock hauls, Temu-dupe rating videos, DIY taba pours, mystery-box unboxings, "squishy sharing" trend, Moriah-style makeovers.
- Hashtags/keywords: #squishy, #needoh, #needohnicecube, #tabasquishy (~126K posts), #dumplingsquishy (>500M views reported), #capybara (billions), "most viral squishies in 2026", "who started the 2026 squishy trend".
- Price bands that convert on TikTok Shop: $2–12 single squishies, $8–13 mystery boxes, $20–30 multipacks; free shipping is table stakes.

## 4. Adjacent trends worth borrowing

Kidult (≈25% of US toy sales; 81% of parents buying toys for themselves), Toy Association 2026 trends "Forever Young", "Cozy Culture" (low/no-tech comfort play), "Fan-Driven Play"; Toy Fair 2026 floor: nearly every brand had a blind-box/mini collectible; slime searches +35% QoQ, DIY slime supplies +115%. Adult sensory/ADHD framing is legitimizing desk squishies (fidget market ~$7.3B 2024 → ~$19.6B 2032). Capybara + cottagecore + "calm" aesthetics.

## 5. Q4 2026 / 2027 forecaster signals

Toy Insider Holiday 2026 (list live 23 Sep): NeeDoh, Squishmallows, KPop Demon Hunters, Bluey, Little Mermaid, Super Mario Galaxy Movie, Toy Story 5, Hoppers. HKTDC "Toys 2027": licensed characters + must-have collectibles. Made-in-China/analyst chatter about "smart" or AR squishies is speculative. Circana notes price mix shifting to mid/premium bands while entry price points are squeezed — argues for $12–30 curated sets over $3 singles.

## 6. Customization landscape

- **Photo/face squishies**: Soufeel, CustWit, CustomCardVerse sell TPR sand-filled "custom face squishy dolls" and squishy-belly fridge magnets (prices not retrievable; comparable Soufeel items run ~$20–35). Etsy: "custom squishy toys", "dog squishy", pet-replica squishies, hand-painted PU customs (typically $15–45).
- **Custom mochi/keychains**: OEM factories (EDNTOY, SentiaPlay, Chung Jen, CPS Toys, SUKEAUTO) quote MOQ 200–1,000 pcs per design, ~1 week sample + 3–4 weeks production; mochi customs land ~$0.30–0.80/pc at 1,000+.
- **Gap**: nobody dominant is doing *pet-face taba/PU squishies* or name-printed taba charms at DTC scale — evidence is thin but the white space is real.

## 7. Ranked shortlist — best 5 bets for a Q4 2026 launch

1. **Silicone "taba"-style squishies with capybara + food designs (curated + mystery-bag format).** Rising, not peaked; Toy Fair validation; wholesale $0.20–2 vs $8–15 retail; TABASQUISHY offers 20-unit MOQs so you can test SKUs. Sell a $12–15 "mystery taba" bag to import the blind-box dopamine RMS proved with Mystery Dumpling. Risk: ORB is already in Walmart, so differentiate on curation, packaging and content.
2. **Personalized pet-face / name squishies (PU or TPR).** Highest defensibility and margin (~$25–40 retail on $3–6 COGS), gift-season timing, immune to Temu price war. Needs a print-on-PU or hand-paint workflow and a 5–7-day promise; start with Etsy-style made-to-order, scale via an OEM at 200-pc MOQ.
3. **Jumbo/giant food squishies (bun, cheese, croissant, 25 cm) as "hero" content props.** $20–35 retail, $3–8 landed; scarce at big-box; ideal for ASMR/unboxing clips and for bundling with minis. Choose PU foam, not gel.
4. **Kidult "desk calm" bundles**: 2–3 taba/PU squishies + 3D-printed articulated fidget or magnetic slug + scented option, positioned for adult ADHD/stress and office gifting at $29–39. Rides Circana's mid-tier price shift and the Cozy Culture/Forever Young narratives; avoids being "just another squishy shop."
5. **Squishy bag charms / keychains (capybara, food, unlicensed kawaii) + squishy 3D phone cases.** Wearable = always-on marketing; $8–15 charms on $0.50–1.50 cost; taps the post-Labubu bag-charm habit without Labubu's fade. Do not touch grey-market Sanrio/Stitch/Pokémon.

**Avoid / de-prioritize**: unbranded gel/water-bead "NeeDoh dupes" and maltose jelly drops (CPSC seizures, recalls, class action, TikTok burn stories); bulk mochi packs; pop-its; Labubu-alikes.

**Honest caveats**: (a) many price/cost figures are from supplier marketing snippets, not invoices; (b) TikTok view counts are self-reported by sellers; (c) the whole category may cool sharply in H1 2027 the way slime/fidget spinners did — plan inventory for Q4 sell-through, not a 12-month runway; (d) Squishable-specific 2026 data and hard Google Trends indices were not obtained.
