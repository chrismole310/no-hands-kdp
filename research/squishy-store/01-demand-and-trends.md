# Squishy Toy Demand: Market Research Brief (as of September 2026)

**Method note:** ~40 web searches. Full-text fetches were blocked on most source domains, so figures come from search-result summaries of those pages. Treat exact figures as "reported by" rather than independently verified. Syndicated market-research vendor numbers (Cognitive, Business Research Insights, Fortune BI, etc.) are low-rigor estimates and conflict with one another; Circana point-of-sale data is the most reliable series here.

---

## 1. Headline finding

**Squishy demand is not just rising in 2026; it is the single hottest toy craze of the year, per Circana POS data.** US squishy-toy sales hit ~$297M in H1 2026, ~4x H1 2025, on 58.6M units (+~200%). Driver: Schylling's NeeDoh (retail ~$5) going viral on TikTok in late 2025/early 2026, plus "butter", "dumpling" and "taba" slow-rising squishies. Two cautions: (a) this is the pattern of a fad (the 2017-18 squishy craze also crossed $100M US then collapsed under knockoffs), and (b) plush, including Squishmallows, is *declining* in the same period, so "squishy" (tactile fidget/sensory) and "squishy plush" are diverging.

**Squishmallows / Jazwares (Berkshire Hathaway):**
- Cumulative units: 100M (Oct 2021) → 400M (Aug 2024) → 485M+ entering 2025, across 3,000+ designs. ~100M units sold in a single year around 2022-23. Jazwares sales topped $1B with Squishmallows ~40% of revenue (CNBC 2023; Built In).
- **2025 was a sharp down year.** Berkshire 10-Qs: Jazwares revenue down 38.5% YTD H1 2025 vs H1 2024 (lower volumes, tariff uncertainty delaying orders). FY2025 10-K: consumer products group revenue $14.4B, -3.0%, decline "primarily due to Fruit of the Loom, Jazwares and Duracell." Circana: plush peaked in 2024 (Squishmallows-led); plush -4% H1 2025 and among the sharpest-declining categories for FY2025.
- **2026 partial recovery:** Q1 2026 Jazwares still a revenue drag; Q2 2026 10-Q reports revenue and earnings gains at Jazwares (higher sales, better gross margin, tariff refunds). No brand-level dollar figures are disclosed.
- Social: #Squishmallows >13B TikTok views (May 2024); one 2025 source claims >25B (unverified).

**Kidult share (Circana):**
- 2025 US: adults 18+ were the fastest-growing, highest-spending cohort, +18% YoY, ~one-quarter of US toy sales (Retail Dive cites ~$9.1B / ~one-fifth; Circana release says ~25%; the two are not fully reconciled). Q1 2025 alone: $1.8B, +12%.
- Global: kidults (12+) = 28% of global toy sales (+2.5 pts since 2022); 15+ nearly 20% and more than doubled since 2020; adults 18+ ~30% / >$12B globally (Circana via Toy Association).
- H1 2026 US: adult 18+ sales +25%, largest contributor to growth; adults drove ~35% of growth; adult-only households = 55% of toy sales (+16%).
- UK: 43% of adults bought a toy for themselves/another adult in 2025 (76% of 18-34s); kidults ~1 in 3 pounds spent (Circana UK, market £3.9B, +3%).

**What happened after 2018:** saturation with cheap non-slow-rising knockoffs by 2019, EU/Danish chemical-safety scrutiny, and the attention shift to Squishmallows (2020-22) and pop-its (2021). Squishies never disappeared (Moriah Elizabeth's "Squishy Makeover" series kept a 10M-subscriber, 4.3B-view channel alive) but retail dollars fell far below the 2018 level until the 2025-26 revival.

## 5. Seasonality

- US hobby/toy/game stores: 36% of 2025 revenue in Oct-Dec; Nov+Dec = 27% (Circana/Census via ClickPost).
- Amazon toys: "most seasonal category"; giftable toy SKUs can do up to 65% of annual sales in the 10-week Q4 window; sales ramp Oct through ~2nd week Dec (AMZScout / seller guides). Prime Day (July) and back-to-school (Aug) are secondary bumps; fidgets are explicitly merchandised as back-to-school by Target/Amazon and as Easter basket stuffers by Walmart/Dollar Tree, but no Circana Easter-week percentage was found.
- Squishy-specific 2026 anomaly: the NeeDoh surge peaked in **March-May 2026** (Faire searches jumped in March; Nebraska GT peak Mar 29), i.e., a non-Q4, social-driven spike, with Google interest for Squishmallows spiking Feb 2026 and May 2026.
- Latin America: December alone = 32% of annual toy sales (Circana).

## 6. Who buys

- **NeeDoh purchasers (Circana, 2026):** 60% parents buying for kids; 18.2% buying for themselves; largest recipient group is children 7-12; **72% of recipients female**.
- **Squishmallows:** one source claims 65% of buyers are 18-24 (unverified marketing stat); adults >20% of plush sales in 2024 (Fortune BI); Discord/Reddit hunting-and-trading communities.
- **Kidults broadly:** see Section 2; motivations cited are stress relief, affordable entertainment, nostalgia, social connection.
- **Price points / AOV:** NeeDoh ~$5; mass-market foam squishies $3-10; Squishmallows $7-30 by size (Walmart/Costco listings, Costco 8-pack 5" for ~$30); Circana industry ASP +4% in H1 2026. No category-specific AOV data was found; basket-stuffer pricing implies multi-unit orders.

## 7. 2025-2026 news: rising or falling?

- **Rising (tactile squishies):** CNBC (Aug 27 2026) "The toy industry's latest craze is cheap, colorful and very squishy"; Circana June 2026 release: sensory toys posting triple-digit growth in dollars and units; "Explorative & Other" supercategory +36% through April and +58% in H1, led by NeeDoh and MLB; Today.com/NBC "NeeDoh hunting" stockouts within hours of restock; KMUW (Aug 21 2026) frames it as the latest in the Beanie Babies / fidget spinner lineage and notes adult scalpers reselling at 2x.
- **Falling (squishy plush):** Plush and traditional dolls declining in H1 2026 even as the industry grows 17%; Squishmallows-led plush peaked 2024; Jazwares revenue -38.5% H1 2025 before a Q2 2026 rebound.
- **Labubu / blind box:** Pop Mart 2025 revenue >$4B; The Monsters (Labubu) RMB 14.16B in 2025 (+366%, 38% of revenue). H1 2026: total revenue RMB 17.17B (+23.8%) but The Monsters fell to RMB 4.45B (26% share), overseas revenue -11.1%, Americas -16.5%; Morningstar: "the earlier frenzy surrounding Labubu has cooled." Relevance: it validated the adult-collector, drop/scarcity, blind-box playbook now being applied to squishies (rare NeeDoh variants, "hunting" videos), and it shows how quickly a social-driven collectible can roll over (~12-18 months from peak).
- **Safety headwind:** UK Trading Standards found many viral dumpling/butter/NeeDoh-style squishies non-compliant with Toy Safety Regulations 2011; knockoff quality ended the 2018 cycle too.

**Bottom line:** As of Sept 2026, squishy/sensory fidget demand is at an all-time high on every measurable axis (POS dollars, units, search, wholesale search, stockouts), while Squishmallows-style plush is in a post-peak decline with early stabilization. Base case for a new entrant: strong Q4 2026, then material risk of a 2027 fade similar to 2019 unless the product is differentiated on texture/IP/collectibility.
