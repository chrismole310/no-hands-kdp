**1688 vs Alibaba:** the same item is often 30–60% cheaper on 1688 (example: $8 Alibaba → $3–5 on 1688), but agents charge 3–10% of order value plus consolidation/shipping [11].

**Freight (Sept 2026):** air freight ~$6.91/kg (≥1,000 kg spot) or **DDP air $4–$8/kg**; ocean **LCL ~$137/cbm** ($100–$150/cbm DDP); air 2–3 days transit, LCL 10–17 days port-to-port plus 1–2 weeks clearance/inland [12]. Squishies are light but bulky — for jumbo foam, volume, not weight, drives cost.

## 2. Tariffs, de minimis, dropshipping

**De minimis is gone:** ended for China/HK May 2, 2025 and for all countries Aug 29, 2025 (EO 14324); CBP made the suspension indefinite in June 2026, statutory repeal July 1, 2027 [13]. Every parcel now pays duty + entry fees; a $4–5 landed dropship item in 2024 lands at **$7–10+** today [13].

**Tariff stack on HTS 9503 toys from China (as of July 24, 2026):**
- MFN base: 0%
- Section 301 List 4A: +7.5%
- Section 301 "forced-labor" action (effective July 24, 2026; China in the 12.5% tier): +12.5%
- **Total ≈ 20%** ad valorem [14][15][16].
- History: IEEPA fentanyl/reciprocal tariffs (which had pushed China toys to ~37.5%+) were struck down by the Supreme Court Feb 20, 2026; a 10% Section 122 tariff ran Feb 24–July 24, 2026, then was replaced by the 12.5% action. Refund claims for IEEPA duties paid Feb 2025–Feb 2026 are pending in ACE [14][15].
- Add Merchandise Processing Fee (0.3464%, ~$33 min on formal entries) and a broker fee of roughly $100–$150 per entry **[est.]**.

**Landed-cost effect:** on a $4.80 FOB bundle, duty adds ~$0.96; on a $2.00 FOB single, ~$0.40. Not fatal at 20% — the bigger cost is that small dropship parcels each eat entry fees.

**Dropshipping in 2026:** CJPacket China→US 8–17 days (1–5 days processing + transit); CJ US warehouse 2–4 days; Zendrop US-warehouse average 8.5 days; Zendrop $49–$79/mo, AutoDS $19.90–$73.90/mo; CJ is free [17][18]. Typical squishy dropship landed cost: **$2–3 product + $4–7 shipping + duty ≈ $7–11 per single unit [est.]** — which is why single-item dropshipping at $12.99 no longer works.

## 5. Marketing benchmarks

- **Meta:** median DTC CPM **$13.52** (IQR $11–$23), CPC $0.57–$0.87, YoY CPM +20%; median ecommerce ROAS **1.93x–2.19x**; Q4 CPMs +35–50% [34][35][36]. Toys/Art/Collectibles was one of the few verticals where CPA improved (−1.5%) Aug 2025–Jul 2026 [37].
- **TikTok Ads:** conversion-objective CPM **$9–$13**, CPC $0.74–$1.50, median ecommerce CPA **$32.74**; Toys/Art/Collectibles CVR 2.38% (among the highest); Q4 +40–80% [38][39].
- **Break-even ROAS** = 1 ÷ contribution margin %: 40% CM → 2.5x, 30% → 3.3x, 25% → 4.0x [40]. A $30 AOV store needs >3x on Meta whose median is <2.2x.
- **Email/SMS:** campaign revenue/recipient $0.18–$0.30; flows ~18x campaign RPR and ~41% of email revenue from 5% of sends; top decile brands get 38–45% of revenue from email+SMS [41].
- **Creators:** UGC video $100–$500 (no posting 40–60% cheaper; Spark/whitelisting rights +25–100%); micro-influencer TikTok post $150–$800 typical, $30–$400 at the nano end [42][43].
- **TikTok Shop affiliate:** 10–15% open collaboration is the norm; 15% commission ≈ 26.6% of net revenue once fees/returns stack [32].

## 7. Worked examples

**Shared assumptions:** self-fulfilled, free shipping to customer, Shopify Basic (2.9% + $0.30), USPS GA commercial blended $6.80, duty 20% on FOB, air-DDP freight ~$6/kg, entry/broker fixed cost $250 per shipment amortized over the run, returns/damage allowance 4% of revenue, poly mailer + insert $0.35.

### A) $29.99 three-pack bundle (1 taba $3.00 + 1 jumbo PU $1.20 + 1 mochi $0.30 + cards/polybags $0.30 = FOB $4.80)

| Line | $ | % of price |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 29.99 | 100% |
| Landed COGS (4.80 FOB + 0.96 duty + 1.50 freight + 0.25 entry) | (7.51) | 25.0% |
| Packaging | (0.35) | 1.2% |
| USPS Ground Advantage | (6.80) | 22.7% |
| Payment processing | (1.17) | 3.9% |
| Returns/damage 4% | (1.20) | 4.0% |
| **Contribution before CAC** | **12.96** | **43.2%** |
| Break-even ROAS (paid orders) | 2.31x | |

| Blended CAC scenario | CAC | Contribution/order | CM % |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: strong organic TikTok/affiliate, paid ≈5x ROAS blended | $5 | **$7.96** | 26.5% |
| B: typical, paid ≈3x, 60–70% of orders paid | $10 | **$2.96** | 9.9% |
| C: Meta-median (~2x ROAS) | $15 | **−$2.04** | −6.8% |

Add a 3PL (~$3/order + $250 minimum) and Scenario B goes negative. Charging $4.95 shipping restores ~$5/order but toy CVR typically falls.

**Channel variants for the same bundle:** TikTok Shop (6% referral $1.80 + 13% affiliate $3.90 in lieu of ad CAC, self-ship $6.80): contribution ≈ **$8.43 (28%)** — the affiliate model outperforms Meta Scenario B because commission is only paid on conversions. Amazon FBA (15% referral $4.50 + FBA ~$3.90 + storage/inbound $0.60): **$11.93 before PPC (40%)**, ~$5.90 after 20% ACoS.

### B) $12.99 single item (jumbo slow-rising, FOB $2.00 incl. card)

| Line | $ | % |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 12.99 | 100% |
| Landed COGS (2.00 + 0.40 duty + 0.60 freight + 0.10 entry) | (3.10) | 23.9% |
| Packaging | (0.35) | 2.7% |
| USPS Ground Advantage | (6.80) | **52.3%** |
| Payment processing | (0.68) | 5.2% |
| Returns 4% | (0.52) | 4.0% |
| **Contribution before CAC** | **$1.54** | **11.9%** |
| Break-even ROAS | **8.4x** | |
| CAC $5 / $10 / $15 | −$3.46 / −$8.46 / −$13.46 | all negative |

The single item loses money on every paid order and nets $1.54 even on a free organic order. Shipping alone is 52% of price — USPS's July 2026 elimination of the 4 oz/8 oz tiers made this ~$2 worse per parcel. Charging $4.95 shipping lifts contribution to ~$6.40 on $17.94 collected, but you are then a $18 single-squishy store competing with $8–12 30-packs on Amazon/TikTok. Conclusion: sell bundles (3+ units, $25–40) or the model fails; use singles only as add-on upsells that ride in an existing parcel (incremental cost ≈ COGS + $0.70, i.e., ~75% margin).

### C) Break-even monthly orders (fixed costs $39 Shopify + ~$60 apps + ~$1.30 domain ≈ $100/mo)

| Case | Contribution/order | Orders to cover $100 fixed | Orders for $3,000/mo owner pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bundle, CAC $5 | $7.96 | **13** | ~390 (13/day) |
| Bundle, CAC $10 | $2.96 | **34** | ~1,050 (35/day) |
| Bundle, CAC $15 | −$2.04 | never | never |
| Bundle via TikTok Shop affiliate | $8.43 | 12 | ~370 |
| Single item, free shipping, 100% organic | $1.54 | 65 | ~2,000 |
| Single item with any paid CAC | negative | never | never |

**Bottom line:** at a $30 AOV the store works only if (a) landed COGS ≤ 25% of price via bulk Alibaba/1688 import (not per-order dropshipping post-de-minimis), (b) every parcel stays under 1 lb and ships at commercial USPS rates, (c) blended CAC stays under ~$8, which realistically means TikTok organic/affiliate rather than Meta prospecting at 2x ROAS, and (d) AOV is pushed toward $35–40 with a free-shipping threshold and one-click add-ons. Grow plan is not worth it below ~$30k/mo (0.2% savings on processing = $60/mo).
