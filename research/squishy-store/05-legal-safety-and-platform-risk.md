# Squishy-Toy Shopify Store: Legal, Safety & Platform Risk Report (US, Sept 2026)

Method note: 28 web searches were run; outbound fetches to CPSC.gov, eCFR, court dockets and several trade sites were blocked by the session proxy, so a few items below rely on search snippets and are flagged "verify". Not legal advice; a product-safety lawyer and a CPSC-accepted lab are needed before launch.

## 1. Trademark / trade dress

**Squishmallows (Kelly Toys / Jazwares, Berkshire-owned).** Registered word mark SQUISHMALLOW (Reg. 5454574, filed 2017) plus "ORIGINAL SQUISHMALLOWS" (Ser. 90676140). Kelly Toys is one of the most aggressive plush enforcers:
- Build-A-Bear "Skoosherz" (filed Jan/Feb 2024, C.D. Cal.; BAB countersued in E.D. Mo.). Settled and dismissed with prejudice; terms undisclosed. Bloomberg Law reports the stipulation Aug 22, 2025 (one secondary source says May 2026). Importantly, no court ever ruled whether the Squishmallows trade dress is protectable.
- Zuru "Snackles" (2:23-cv-09255, C.D. Cal., filed Nov 2, 2023) — sent to mediation by July 2025; final outcome not found (verify on PACER).
- Ty Inc. and Dan-Dee International — trade dress/copyright claims asserted (status not found).
- Alibaba (S.D.N.Y. 22-cv-09384) — motion to dismiss denied Dec 2023 (contributory infringement for hosting counterfeit sellers).
- 135+ online sellers, Schedule A case, N.D. Ill., filed Jan 5, 2024 (TROs, frozen marketplace balances are standard in these cases).
- Kelly Toys itself is now a defendant: Kidrobot's HUGME vs Jazwares "HugMees," May 27, 2026 ruling lets the case proceed — a reminder that word-mark similarity is litigated in both directions.

Claimed trade dress (from the complaints): egg/bell-shaped body, "Asian-style kawaii" embroidered faces, simple two-tone coloring with contrasting belly, ultra-soft velvety "spandex-ey" fabric, and individual character names/bios. **Avoid:** any "squishmallow", "squish-mallow", "mallow" naming; egg-shaped plush with embroidered kawaii face and belly patch; character bio cards; comparative ads ("like Squishmallows").

**"Squishies"** — widely treated as generic for foam squeeze toys; Kellytoy's own "Squishy Squooshems" registration was cancelled in 2012, and a 2017 application "SQUISHIES" (Ser. 87655056, LI LU RONG) covers toys but its status should be checked on TSDR before relying on it. Practically safe to use descriptively ("slow-rising squishies"), not as your brand.

**"Squishable"** — live registered mark (Reg. 3685324, 2009, renewed 2019, Class 28 plush) owned by Squishable.com, plus SQUISHLINGS. Do not use "Squishable(s)" as a product name or SEO keyword in a source-identifying way.

**"Mochi squishy"** — no registration found; it is a descriptive material/style term. Low risk when used descriptively; do not try to register it.

**"Pop It"** — FoxMind owns POP IT! (Reg. 6183005, "bubble popping games"). FoxMind ran a Schedule A suit against Amazon sellers (S.D.N.Y. 1:21-cv-05136) with account freezes; a 2024 declaratory action by Big Time Toys (M.D. Tenn. 3:24-cv-01007) challenges the mark. Call bubble-poppers "push-bubble fidget" or "silicone popper" until that is resolved.

## 2. Copyright / licensed characters

Unlicensed Sanrio (Hello Kitty, Cinnamoroll), Pokémon, Disney Stitch, and Nintendo Kirby squishies from Alibaba are counterfeit as a matter of law even if the seller was unaware. Enforcement pattern: Schedule A suits in N.D. Ill. (Nintendo filed a new one Sept 4, 2026, 1:26-cv-10730; Sesame Workshop sued over marketplace counterfeits; IPWatchdog Sept 17, 2026 confirms the tactic is expanding), ex parte TROs, and freezing of Amazon/TikTok Shop/PayPal balances. Shopify's AUP and Payments ToS both prohibit infringing goods; a DMCA/trademark notice yields content removal and repeat-infringer termination, and most Shopify store shutdowns are Payments-side. TikTok Shop: listing removal, suspension, withheld payouts. **Mitigation:** only original designs or items with a written license from the rights holder; keep supplier authorization letters; never source "Sanrio-style" from Alibaba.

## 3. Product safety (CPSC)

- Squishies are toys for children under 14; anything designed/intended primarily for age ≤12 is a **children's product** requiring third-party testing at a CPSC-accepted lab, a **Children's Product Certificate (CPC)**, and permanent **tracking labels** (importer name, location, month/year, batch — 16 CFR 1130; hangtags/stickers do not count).
- **ASTM F963-23** is mandatory for toys made on/after April 20, 2024 (physical/mechanical, flammability, 8 soluble heavy metals, and expanding-materials limits that drove the 2026 water-bead recalls).
- **Small parts:** 16 CFR 1501 bans small parts (fits 1.25 in x 2.25 in cylinder) in toys for under 3; 16 CFR 1500.19 requires "WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD — Small parts. Not for children under 3 yrs." for ages 3–6, and 1500.20 requires the warning in online listings. Most mochi squishies are themselves a small part, so **age-grade 3+ minimum, never market for toddlers**.
- **Phthalates:** 16 CFR 1307 — eight phthalates each ≤0.1% (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIBP, DPENP, DHEXP, DCHP) in any plasticized part; PVC/TPR mochi squishies are the highest-risk material. **Lead:** 90 ppm surface coatings, 100 ppm substrate.
- **Danish EPA (June 2018):** all 12 PU-foam squishies tested emitted dimethylformamide, styrene, toluene and other VOCs at "very high" levels; BR, Top-Toy and Toys"R"Us Denmark pulled them (an advisory, not a formal ban — Snopes). US law has **no VOC/DMF emission limit** in ASTM F963, so this is a tort/reputation risk, and DMF, toluene and styrene are all Prop 65-listed chemicals.
- **US recalls/enforcement 2026:** GIHNJSI "Squeezy Dumplings" (Sept 3, 2026, ~8,000 units, Amazon, water beads) and OKK Trading "Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun" (~7,200 units) — combined ~15,200; CPSC Consumer Safety Alert (Sept 17, 2026) on fake/counterfeit squishies: 55 shipments/355,683 units stopped at ports, notices of violation issued, plus the microwave-squishy burn trend. No 2018–2025 US squishy recall found for VOCs.
- **Testing cost:** roughly $420–600 per SKU for ASTM F963-23 physical/mechanical at budget accredited labs; full CPSIA chemistry scales per material/color (each colorway is a test point). Realistic budget $800–2,400 per SKU, less with component-part testing across shared materials.
- **"Ages 14+ / adult collectible" route:** 16 CFR 1200.2 uses four factors — manufacturer's statement of intended age (only if reasonable), packaging/marketing, whether consumers commonly recognize it as for ≤12, and the CPSC Age Determination Guidelines (Jan 2020). CPSC has said a label alone is not dispositive; a cute, squeezable, low-priced item with play value is a children's product regardless of a 14+ sticker. The rule's "collectible" carve-out targets genuinely adult items (high price, display, fragile, sold in adult channels). For squishies this route is **not legitimate** and CPSC's 2026 alert shows they are actively screening this exact category. Get a lawyer's opinion only if you sell high-priced artist/designer pieces.

## 4. Import / customs

- HTS 9503.00.00 (toys) base rate free. For China in Sept 2026: Section 301 List 4A 7.5% plus the Section 301 forced-labor tariff (12.5%, effective July 24, 2026, replacing the expired 10% Section 122 tariff) — about **20% total** per trade trackers; IEEPA "reciprocal" tariffs were struck down by the Supreme Court Feb 20, 2026. **Verify with a broker at time of entry; rates changed four times in 2026.**
- De minimis ended for China/HK May 2, 2025 and for all countries Aug 29, 2025. Shipments ≤$2,500 use informal Type 11 (bond usually not required); >$2,500 formal Type 01 needs a licensed broker and a bond (continuous bond roughly $400–600/yr, verify). Because toys need a CPC, **CPSC eFiling in ACE is mandatory since July 8, 2026** (PGA message set at entry) — in practice this means using a broker even for small shipments. Postal informal-entry process began July 24, 2026 (PGA compliance Oct 22, 2026).
- **Prop 65:** DEHP/DBP/BBP/DINP, DMF, toluene, styrene all listed; short-form warning must now name a chemical (rules effective Jan 1, 2025, transition to Jan 1, 2028) and must appear on the product page for online sales. Prop 65 bounty-hunter notices against toys are routine.

## 5. Platform rules

- **Shopify Payments:** toys are not restricted; adult toys, weapons, and IP-infringing goods are prohibited. Sudden viral volume (TikTok) can trigger reserves/reviews; keep CPCs and supplier invoices on hand.
- **TikTok Shop US:** Toys & Hobby requirements (June 19, 2026): CPC ≤365 days old, CPSC-accepted lab report ≤365 days, Certificate of Conformity ≤2 years, from manufacturer/importer/repacker.
- **Amazon:** Toys category approval requires CPC, test reports, images of tracking label and choking warnings, and clean supplier invoices (retail receipts rejected); holiday seller-fulfilled gating: 25 SFP orders Aug–Oct and first order before Sept (FBA exempt).

## 6. Advertising to children

- Amended COPPA rule effective June 23, 2025; compliance deadline April 22, 2026; separate verifiable parental consent for targeted ads; no data collection from under-13s on your site (no kid-facing quizzes/newsletters).
- Meta (April 2026 global Teen Accounts): under-18 targeting limited to age and location; no interests/lookalikes/retargeting. TikTok US: no personalized targeting under 18; only location/language/device, and ads must meet Teen Safety policy. Target parents/collectors 18+ and keep creative FTC-truthful (no "non-toxic" claims without a test report).

## Launch compliance checklist

1. Trademark clearance (TSDR + Google) on brand and every product name; avoid "mallow", "Squishable", "Pop It".
2. Design review against Squishmallows trade dress; document your own creative process.
3. Supplier contract: IP indemnity, phthalate-free/lead-free material declaration, no water beads, ASTM F963-23 compliance clause.
4. Age-grade each SKU (≥3 for anything that fits the small-parts cylinder); do not rely on "14+".
5. Pre-production samples to a CPSC-accepted lab: ASTM F963-23, CPSIA lead (paint/substrate), 16 CFR 1307 phthalates, small parts; optional VOC screen for PU foam.
6. Issue a CPC per SKU (importer is certifier); retest per material change and at least annually (TikTok Shop requires ≤365 days).
7. Permanent tracking label on product (or on packaging if product too small — document why).
8. Choking-hazard warning on principal display panel and every online listing (16 CFR 1500.20).
9. Prop 65 short-form warning naming a chemical on pack and product page.
10. Customs: broker engaged, HTS 9503.00.00 confirmed, bond if formal entry, CPSC Product Registry account for eFiling.
11. Platform files: upload CPC, test reports, label/warning photos to Amazon Toys and TikTok Shop Toys & Hobby before listing.
12. Marketing: 18+ ad targeting; no child data collection; substantiate "non-toxic"/"BPA-free" claims with reports.
13. Recall plan: batch records tied to tracking labels; CPSC Section 15(b) reporting awareness.
14. Insurance: product liability policy ($1M) naming marketplaces as additional insured.

**Where a professional is required:** product-safety counsel for age-grading opinion and Prop 65 wording; IP counsel for a clearance opinion; a CPSC-accepted lab for all testing; a licensed customs broker for entry and eFiling.
