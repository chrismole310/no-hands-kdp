# Same Bones — One-Page Brief

**Tagline:** Same bones. New world.
**Channel:** youtube.com/@samebones · samebonesofficial@gmail.com
**Thesis:** You are running a 200,000-year-old body in a world it was never built for. Every video explains one strange thing humans do, where it came from, and what it was for.

---

## What it is
A stick-figure explainer channel in the Ink Explainer style: one recurring ink-line figure, one narrator voice, burned captions, 9 to 11 minute episodes, sources cited in every description. Three Shorts cut from each episode. Everything auto-produced through the Higgsfield faceless-video pipeline and scheduled through Zernio to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

## Why now
- Ink Explainer: 16 videos, 5 months, 15.5M views, 104K subs. Its 9.4M video is 11 minutes, second-person, sourced.
- Sub-5K-subscriber channels in this style still landed 300K to 940K view videos in August and September 2026. The algorithm is feeding the format.
- The exact "ancient humans" topic is crowded: 80 look-alike channels created since April. The *style* is not crowded. Adjacent lanes (psychology, body, everyday logic, survival simulation) have proven 2M to 12M hits and a fraction of the competition.

## The rule
Every video must be about the human. If the title doesn't answer "why do *you* do, feel, fear, or survive this?", it doesn't go on Same Bones. Space, factories, celebrities, and gaming are out unless the human is the subject.

## Lanes and formats

First 30 videos stay in three lanes: ancient, body, mind. Widen later through series, never through style.

## Script formula (from 10 top transcripts)
Second-person present-tense scene in sentence one → the obvious question nobody asks → "most people assume… but" → explicit promise → labelled zones or numbered items, one concrete number per beat → return to the opening image with new meaning. 2,000 to 2,400 words, 200+ words per minute, no CTA until the last ten seconds.

## Cadence and posting
- Bank ten episodes, then release one every 48 hours via the YouTube scheduler. Strongest ancient-humans video first.
- One Short a day, each linked to its episode with the related-video field.
- Same-day native posts to Facebook and Instagram Reels through Zernio. Different audience, no cannibalization.
- Steady state: three episodes a week, one Short a day, forever.

## Production
Higgsfield faceless-video, Stickman Cartoon preset, still-picture mode by default with motion blocks on the hook and ending. Locked asset roster: one figure, five props, one voice. Higgsfield thumbnail workflow, two to three thumbnails per video for YouTube's test-and-compare. Zernio API as the last pipeline step.

## Money
- YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours. Estimated 60 to 70 percent chance inside 90 days, 80 percent inside six months, if posting never stops. All comparable channels are monetized.
- RPM $2 to $4. 2M monthly views ≈ $4K to $8K. Facebook and Instagram add 20 to 40 percent.
- The business is the back-end: KDP books via No Hands KDP, then a course or series bundle. Every strong episode is a chapter.

## What kills it
Copying a competitor's title. Off-thesis videos. Style drift between episodes. Stopping after ten. Padding past 12 minutes. Videos under 8 minutes (no mid-rolls).

## First 30 days
Week 1: pipeline test, asset roster, episode one. Weeks 2–3: ten episodes scheduled, thirty Shorts. Week 4: read CTR and retention per lane, double down on the winner, keep ancient humans at one in four.
